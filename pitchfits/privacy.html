This Privacy Policy explains what information PitchFits ("we", "us") collects when you visit PitchFits.com or place an order, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email, phone number, shipping and billing address, and order details when you check out, contact us or join our newsletter.
  • Payment information: payments are processed directly by our payment providers (such as Shopify Payments, Stripe, PayPal, Klarna, Afterpay and Affirm). We do not see or store your full card number.
  • Automatic information: device and browser type, IP address, pages viewed and how you reached our site, collected through cookies and similar technologies.

2. How we use your information

  • To process, ship and deliver your orders, and send order and shipping updates.
  • To answer your questions and handle returns, exchanges and refunds.
  • To send marketing emails if you subscribed — you can unsubscribe at any time with one click.
  • To prevent fraud and keep our website secure.
  • To understand how our website is used and improve it.

3. How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run our store, and only as needed:

  • Payment processors, to take payments securely.
  • Our fulfillment partners and shipping carriers (USPS, UPS, FedEx), to pack and deliver your order.
  • Our e-commerce platform, email and analytics providers.
  • Authorities, when required by law or to protect our rights.

4. Cookies

We use cookies to keep items in your cart, remember your preferences and understand site traffic. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but some features — like the cart — may not work properly.

5. Your rights

Depending on your state (including California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut and others), you may have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to access or delete it, to correct it, and to opt out of targeted advertising. To make a request, email support@pitchfits.com. We'll respond within 45 days and won't treat you differently for exercising your rights.

6. Data retention & security

We keep order information as long as needed to fulfill your order and meet legal, tax and accounting requirements. We use industry-standard security, including encrypted (SSL) connections, to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we work hard to protect your data.

7. Children

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Jerseys for kids should be purchased by a parent or guardian.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed.

9. Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email support@pitchfits.com.
