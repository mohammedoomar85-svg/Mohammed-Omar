Delivery times

  • Delivery time includes order processing — no hidden extra days.
  • Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding US federal holidays.
  • During busy periods such as Black Friday and the holidays, a small number of orders may take 1–2 extra days. We'll email you if your order is affected.

Where we ship

We ship to every address in the 50 US states, including PO boxes and APO/FPO/DPO military addresses. Orders to Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico and military addresses may take a few extra days. We do not currently ship outside the United States.

Order confirmation & tracking

You'll receive an order confirmation email right after checkout, and a shipping email with your tracking number as soon as your order leaves our warehouse. Track your order here.

Multiple items

If you order several jerseys, they may arrive in separate packages on different days. Each package has its own tracking number.

Wrong address

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. If you spot a mistake, email us within 12 hours of placing your order and we'll update it. We can't change the address once an order has shipped, and we're not responsible for orders delivered to an incorrect address entered at checkout.

Lost or delayed packages

If your order hasn't arrived within 10 business days, or tracking hasn't updated for 5 days, contact us. We'll investigate with the carrier and, if the package is lost, send a replacement or a full refund.

Sales tax

Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on your shipping address, as required by your state.
