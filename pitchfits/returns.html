Not the right fit? You have 30 days from delivery to return or exchange your jersey.

What can be returned

  • Items must be unworn, unwashed and in their original condition, with tags attached and in the original packaging.
  • Jerseys sold "as pictured" with a player's name and number can be returned under these same conditions.

What can't be returned

  • Custom name & number jerseys (your own name or number) are made for you and are final sale — unless they arrive damaged, defective or incorrect.
  • Items that have been worn, washed, altered or damaged after delivery.
  • Items returned more than 30 days after delivery.

How to start a return

  1. Email support@pitchfits.com with your order number, the item(s) you want to return and the reason.
  2. We'll reply within 24 hours with your return address and instructions.
  3. Pack the item securely and ship it with a trackable service. Keep your tracking number.
  4. Once we receive and inspect your return, we'll email you to confirm your refund or exchange.

Please don't send items back without contacting us first — returns sent without approval can't be processed.

Refunds

  • Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method within 3–5 business days of inspection. Your bank or card provider may take 5–10 business days to show it.
  • Orders paid with Klarna, Afterpay or Affirm are refunded through that provider, which adjusts your remaining payments.
  • Original shipping costs are non-refundable. Return shipping is paid by the customer, unless the item arrived damaged, defective or incorrect.

Exchanges

Need a different size? Follow the same steps and tell us the size you'd like. If it's in stock, we'll ship your new size as soon as your return is received. If it isn't, we'll refund you.

Damaged, defective or wrong items

We're sorry! Email us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and photos of the item and label. We'll send a free replacement or a full refund — including shipping — and you won't pay for the return.

Cancellations

You can cancel your order within 12 hours of placing it by emailing us. After that, your order may already be on its way — you can still return it under this policy once it arrives.

Sale items

Jerseys bought at sale prices, including Black Friday, can be returned under this same policy and are refunded at the price you paid.
