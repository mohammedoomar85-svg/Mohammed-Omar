As soon as your order ships, we email you a tracking number and a link to follow your package. Every order is delivered in 5–7 business days anywhere in the USA.

How to track

  1. Check your email. Look for "Your PitchFits order is on the way" — it contains your tracking number. Check your spam or promotions folder too.
  2. Click the tracking link in that email, or enter the tracking number on the carrier's website (USPS, UPS or FedEx).
  3. No email yet? Send us your order number below and we'll reply with your tracking details.
This opens your email app with your request ready to send. We reply within 24 hours.

Tracking says delivered, but no package?

Check around your property, mailbox, with neighbors and your building's front desk — carriers sometimes mark packages delivered up to 24 hours early. Still nothing after 48 hours? Contact us and we'll open a claim with the carrier.
