How to measure

Chest

Measure around the fullest part of your chest, under your arms, keeping the tape level.

Length

Measure from the highest point of the shoulder down to the bottom hem of the shirt.

Best tip

Lay a T-shirt that fits you well flat, measure it, and compare with the table.
