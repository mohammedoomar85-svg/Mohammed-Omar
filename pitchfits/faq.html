Orders & Shipping

How long does delivery take?

Every order is delivered in 5–7 business days anywhere in the USA, with tracking. See our Shipping Policy.

How much is shipping?

Shipping is FREE on orders of $75 or more. Orders under $75 ship for a flat $6.99.

Do you ship outside the USA?

Not at the moment — we ship to all 50 US states, including PO boxes and military addresses.

How do I track my order?

You'll get an email with your tracking number as soon as your order ships. You can also visit our Track Your Order page.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes, within 12 hours of placing it. Email us with your order number and the change you need.

Products & Sizing

How do the jerseys fit?

They have an athletic fit, close to the body. If you're between sizes or like a relaxed fit, size up one. See our Size Guide.

Does the jersey come with the name and number in the photos?

Yes. Choose "As pictured" on the product page and you'll get the jersey exactly as shown, at no extra cost. You can also choose a blank jersey for the same price.

Can I put my own name and number on a jersey?

Yes! Choose "Custom name & number" on any product page for $9.99. Custom jerseys are final sale.

What's the difference between Club Kits and Classic Kits?

Club Kits and National Teams are current kits. Classic Kits are shirts from previous seasons and past tournaments.

How do I wash my jersey?

Turn it inside out, machine wash cold on a gentle cycle and hang to dry. Don't tumble dry, bleach or iron over the name, number or badges.

Payments & Discounts

Which payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Venmo, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Shop Pay and Cash App Pay.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes. Split your order into 4 interest-free payments with Klarna, Afterpay or Affirm at checkout.

Is checkout secure?

Yes. Payments are processed by PCI-compliant payment providers over an encrypted connection. We never see or store your full card number.

How does the Black Friday sale work?

During Black Friday every jersey is $45 instead of $65 — no code needed, the price is already reduced.

Can I use WELCOME10 during Black Friday?

WELCOME10 gives 10% off full-price jerseys. It can't be combined with Black Friday prices, which are already our lowest prices of the year.

Returns

What is your return policy?

You can return unworn items in original condition within 30 days of delivery. See Returns & Refunds.

My item arrived damaged or wrong. What do I do?

Email us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and photos. We'll send a free replacement or a full refund.

How long do refunds take?

We issue approved refunds within 3–5 business days of receiving your return. Your bank may take 5–10 business days to show it.
