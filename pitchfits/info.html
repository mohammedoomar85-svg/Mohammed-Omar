FAQ

How does Buy 2 Get 1 Free work?

Add any 3 items to your cart and the cheapest one becomes free automatically. Add 6 and you get 2 free, and so on.

Can I add a name and number?

Yes. On any product page tick "Add name & number" for $9.99.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Klarna.

My order hasn't arrived yet

Most orders arrive within 7–14 business days. If it's been longer, email support@pitchfits.com and we'll sort it out.
