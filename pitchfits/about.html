PitchFits started with a simple idea: every fan in America should be able to wear the shirt of the team — and the player — they love, without paying $150 for it.

Who we are

We're football fans first. We grew up watching Ronaldinho at the Camp Nou, Neymar at Santos and the World Cup every four years. With the 2026 World Cup played across the United States, football has never been bigger here — and we built PitchFits so fans from Los Angeles to New York can rep their country, their club and their legends.
