# الحلقة ١ · سمّ الله وكل بيمينك — برومبت Manus

ارفق مع البرومبت صور الشخصيات الخمس من `brand/characters/ai`: المجموعة، وفهد، ولولوة، واليدّة، وفزّو.

```
You are the production team for "Fizz & Play" (فز و العب), a Kuwaiti educational cartoon series for children aged 3 to 8. Produce Episode 1: "سمّ الله وكل بيمينك" (Say Bismillah and Eat with Your Right Hand).

CHARACTERS — use the attached reference images and keep every character EXACTLY as shown (face, hair, clothes, colours, proportions) in every image:
- Fahad (فهد): 6-year-old Kuwaiti boy, curly dark brown hair, cream t-shirt with navy trim, jeans, plain navy-and-white sneakers with NO logos, no watch. Energetic and always in a hurry.
- Lulwa (لولوة): 6-year-old Kuwaiti girl, long wavy dark brown hair, yellow bow, long coral-red dress with gold embroidery and gold sash, yellow shoes. Calm, thoughtful, asks good questions.
- Al-Yadda (اليدّة): kind Kuwaiti grandmother, black malfa head scarf, white hair at the front, round gold glasses, green thoub nashal with gold embroidery, henna on her hands, gold bangles, wooden cane. Gentle, warm, never scolds harshly.
- Fizzo (فزّو): ginger tabby Arabian Mau cat, green eyes, teal collar with a gold star tag. Never speaks; he springs up suddenly and makes everyone laugh.

STYLE: professional 2D children's animated series, clean line art, soft cel shading, warm cosy colours, big expressive eyes, the exact style of the attached references. Setting: Al-Yadda's traditional Kuwaiti majlis at lunchtime — floor cushions, a red Sadu-pattern rug, a sufra (floor cloth) with a large tray of chicken machboos, a bowl of salad, laban glasses, sunlight through a window with a palm tree outside. 16:9 frames.

STRICT RULES:
1. Never depict the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, any prophet or any companion in any form — no face, body, silhouette or light figure. When the grandmother quotes him, show only her face, the children listening, or the food.
2. Quote the hadith words exactly as written below. Do not add or change religious content.
3. No written text inside images (subtitles are added in editing). No logos or brand names anywhere.
4. Gentle tone: no fear, no shaming. Mistakes are funny and corrected with love.

THE LESSON (simple words for kids):
- Say "بسم الله" before eating → Allah puts blessing (بركة) in our food, so a little is enough for all of us.
- Forgot at the start? Say "بسم الله أوله وآخره".
- Eat with the right hand → the Prophet ﷺ taught us to, and the right hand is for good and nice things.
- Eat from what is in front of you → so everyone gets their share and the food stays clean and nice for others.
- Finish with "الحمد لله".

SCRIPT (Kuwaiti dialogue — keep the Arabic exactly):

Scene 1 — Lunch is ready (0:00–0:25)
The sufra is laid out. Fahad runs in sniffing the air.
فهد: ريحة مجبوس! أنا يوعان وايد!

Scene 2 — Fahad's rush (0:25–0:55)
Fahad sits and quickly grabs food with his LEFT hand, then reaches across the tray to take the chicken in front of Lulwa. Rice falls on the rug. Lulwa looks surprised.
لولوة: فهد! هذا قدّامي!
Fizzo springs up from behind a cushion and tries to steal a piece of chicken too. Everyone laughs.
اليدّة (تضحك): حتى فزّو صار مثلك يا فهد!

Scene 3 — Al-Yadda's story (0:55–1:40)
Al-Yadda gently holds Fahad's hand. Warm close-up of her face.
اليدّة: تعرفون؟ كان في ولد صغير مثلكم ياكل مع نبيّنا محمد ﷺ، وكانت يده تروح يمين ويسار في الصحن. فقال له النبي ﷺ: «يا غلام، سمِّ الله، وكُلْ بيمينك، وكُلْ مما يليك».
(Show only the grandmother, the children's faces and the tray — never the Prophet or the boy from the story.)

Scene 4 — Three rules, counted on fingers (1:40–2:40)
Al-Yadda raises one finger at a time; the children copy her and count with the viewer.
اليدّة: واحد: نقول «بسم الله» قبل لا ناكل، عشان ربي يحط البركة في أكلنا، ويكفينا كلنا.
لولوة: واحد!
اليدّة: اثنين: ناكل بيدنا اليمين، لأن النبي ﷺ علّمنا كذا، واليمين للأشياء الحلوة.
فهد (يرفع يده اليمين): اثنين!
اليدّة: ثلاثة: ناكل من اللي قدّامنا، عشان كل واحد ياخذ نصيبه، والأكل يظل نظيف للكل.
فهد ولولوة: ثلاثة!

Scene 5 — "I forgot!" (2:40–3:05)
فهد (مستحي): بس يا يدّة… أنا نسيت أقول بسم الله أول ما بديت!
اليدّة (تبتسم): ولا يهمك يا حبيبي. إذا نسيت، قل: «بسم الله أوله وآخره».
فهد: بسم الله أوله وآخره!
He eats with his right hand from in front of him. Lulwa smiles. Fizzo sits politely beside his own small bowl.

Scene 6 — Short song (3:05–3:35)
All three sing, clapping softly; Fizzo bounces to the rhythm:
بسم الله… بسم الله… قبل ما ناكل نقول بسم الله
بيميني… بيميني… وآكل من اللي قدّامي
وإذا خلصنا نقول… الحمد لله، الحمد لله!

Scene 7 — Ending (3:35–3:50)
Everyone finishes eating.
الكل: الحمد لله!
فهد (للمشاهد): وأنت؟ لا تنسى… بسم الله، وبيمينك!
Fizzo springs toward the camera. End card with the "فز و العب" logo.

DELIVERABLES:
1. A storyboard: one 16:9 image per scene (7 images), plus 2 extra close-ups (Fahad's left hand grabbing food; Al-Yadda counting on her henna fingers).
2. A shot list table: scene, duration, camera angle, action, dialogue.
3. Voice-over direction for each character (age, tone, speed) and sound effects list.
4. If you can generate video: a 3:30–4:00 animated episode in 16:9 with Arabic subtitles, and a 60-second vertical (9:16) cut of Scenes 2, 4 and 6 for Instagram and TikTok.
5. A thumbnail: Fahad holding up his right hand, Lulwa smiling, Fizzo jumping, bright and clear.
```
